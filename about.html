Who we are

About.

ASA TUK brings together architecture students at the Technical University of Kenya. We support each other through design studio, share skills that lectures do not cover, and connect students with practising architects and the wider built environment profession.

We believe good architecture in Kenya begins with students who ask hard questions about climate, community, materials and cost.

Our aims.

Learn

Peer teaching, software and model-making skills.

Connect

Links to architects, firms and other universities.

Build

Real projects that serve real communities.

Show

Exhibit student work to the public and industry.
